About Zaib City

Zaib City is a bright 3D city where nobody steals cars: you ask nicely, and drivers hop out, wave and lend you theirs. Explore on foot or by car, earn money driving taxis, delivering pizza and putting out fires, and follow a 22-mission story across the city's districts.

Being a good citizen pays. Your Reputation goes up when you finish jobs, wait at red lights and greet people, and a higher Reputation raises the pay for every job.

3 quick tips

  1. Do the story first. It opens districts, pays well and introduces every job type.
  2. Stop at red lights. Every stop nudges your Reputation up, and Reputation raises all your pay.
  3. Tap the big map to set a waypoint and follow the pink line. It is quicker than exploring blind.

❔ How to play

Zaib City is a friendly open city. Nobody gets hurt: people dodge, bump and laugh it off!

On foot

👆 Left stick: walk (push far to run) · JUMP · ENTER near any car. Drivers happily stop, step out and wave.

Driving

◀ ▶ steer (or tilt / stick in Settings) · GAS · BRAKE (hold to reverse) · DRIFT handbrake · NITRO · 📯 horn (honk near people and they wave!)

Everywhere

Drag the screenDrag the mouse to look around · 📱 phone for jobs, cars, houses and more · 🗺️ map: tapclick to set a GPS waypoint.

Keyboard

WASD / arrows move & drive · Space handbrake / jump · Shift nitro / sprint · E enter/exit · H horn · M map · P pause · C camera · R radio · F spray · Q siren · Tab phone · Enter enter/exit or use a marker

Goal

Help the city! Do story missions (★ on the map), jobs at the coloured markers, find hidden stars, land stunt jumps, buy cars and homes and become the Hero of Zaib City.
